🏆 You stayed alert.  ·  你保持了警觉。

Five safeguards kept people safe today:
🚶Stay in the yellow walkway. Stop and look at crossings.走在黄色人行道内，在路口停下并观察。
🚦Control your speed — the limiter helps, but stopping is on you.控制车速 — 限速器有帮助，但刹车靠你自己。
📢Sound the horn at corners. Make eye contact before you pass.在转角按喇叭，通过前与对方目光确认。
🔄Never walk or drive behind a turning MHE — the tail swings wide.切勿走到或驶到转弯的物料搬运设备后方 — 车尾会大幅外摆。
🔵Respect the beacon and the belt — buckle up, watch the amber light.重视警示灯与安全带 — 系好安全带，留意黄灯。
